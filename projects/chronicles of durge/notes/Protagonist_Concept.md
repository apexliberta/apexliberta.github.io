# Protagonist Concept: The Dark Urge (Human Form)

## Core Identity
- **Original Essence:** The Dark Urge (a primordial, violent force).
- **Current Vessel:** A human teenage boy (approx. 15–17 years old).
- **The Transformation:** Orin destroyed his original form and reshaped him into this human shape. This is an act of both destruction and mockery.

## Physicality & The "Vessel"
- **Appearance:** Looks like a vulnerable, innocent teenage boy.
- **Internal Conflict:** A massive disconnect between the fragile human exterior and the chaotic, violent essence within.
- **Body Horror Elements:** (To be expanded) The sensation of the old self trying to break through the new skin; the "wrongness" of being human.

## Psychological Profile
- **Memory Status:** Damaged/Lost. He is a blank slate or has fragmented memories of his former power.
- **The Struggle:** 
    - Does he crave the return to his old, monstrous self?
    - Can the human mind "tame" the primordial urge?
    - How does he react to the taboo impulses that drive him?

## Key Themes
- **Nature vs. Nurture:** The battle between the innate darkness and the new human experience.
- **Identity & Transformation:** What defines a person—their soul/essence or their physical form?
- **Darkness & Taboo:** Exploring violent, visceral, and morally complex themes through a lens of vulnerability.

## Narrative Questions to Explore
1. Why did Orin choose this specific form for him?
2. How does the world react to him (seeing a boy vs. sensing a monster)?
3. What is the ultimate goal: Reclaiming power or finding peace?
