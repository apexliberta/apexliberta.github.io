# System Design: Advanced Class System

This story utilizes a homebrew progression system where characters evolve through combat experience.

### **1. Base Classes**
The fundamental building blocks of character progression (e.g., Warrior, Cleric, Rogue, Wizard).

### **2. Advanced Classes (Class Evolution)**
Advanced classes are formed by combining two or more Base Classes. This represents a character mastering multiple disciplines through the "gradual opening" of their potential.
*   **Example:** A character with levels in both *Cleric* and *Fighter* evolves into a **Paladin**.

### **3. The Leveling Constraint**
The effective power level of an Advanced Class is dictated by the **lowest** level among its constituent Base Classes. 
*   **Rule:** If a character is a **Level 1 Cleric / Level 3 Fighter**, they access **Level 1 Paladin** abilities, spells, or passives only. This ensures that while the character is versatile, their mastery of complex advanced techniques is limited by their weakest foundation.

### **4. Progression Catalyst: The Gradual Opening**
Leveling is not a sudden surge of power but a gradual internalizing of combat experience and trauma. The presence of the Mind Flayer parasite acts as a conduit, allowing the Protagonist to rapidly absorb lessons from life-or-scale encounters.
