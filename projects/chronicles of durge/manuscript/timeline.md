# Story Timeline

## Phase 1: The Awakening

- **Day 0:** The Nautiloid crash and the descent to the beach. Protagonist wakes up and meets Shadowheart.

## Phase 2: The Training Period (The Three-Month Sanctuary)

- **Month 1 - Month 3:** Residence at Bastian and Elara's farm.
  - Intensive training for the Protagonist.
  - Development of romance with Shadowheart.
  - Skirmishes with Mind Flayers and Intellect Devourers to build strength.
  - The truth about the tadpoles is revealed to Bastian and Elara.

## Phase 3: The Journey to the Emerald Grove

- **Day [A]:** Departure from the farm to maintain independence and protect the couple; they establish their first camp together, just as in the game.
  - Arrival near the Emerald Grove; encounter with the Goblin Raid (Aradin's group).
  - Entry into the Grove; interactions with Zevlor, Kagha, and Nettie.
  - The Rite of Thorns ultimatum is issued by Kagha.
  - Rescue of Arabella.
  - The Sazza incident.
- **Day [B]:** Meeting with Zevlor following the Sazza incident; he pleads for assistance in clearing a path through goblin territory to ensure safe passage toward Baldur's Gate.
  - Intelligence is gathered via a scout, providing vital information on goblin positions.
  - The Protagonist assumes command of a battalion composed of himself, Shadowheart, and tiefling warriors from the Grove to secure the route.

## Phase 4: The Path to the Sunlit Wetlands

- **Day [A]:** Traveling toward the wetlands; discovery of the secret entrance under the Druid Grove.
- **Day [B]:** Clearing the cave and rescuing Findel.
- **Day [C]:** Approaching the Blighted Village; deciding to navigate around it to avoid the goblin ambush.
- **Day [D]:** Encounter with Auntie Ethel in the wetlands; mediation between Ethel and the two men.
- **Day [E]:** The decision to return to the Druid Grove/Refugee camp to gather support for a coordinated assault on Ethel's cottage.
