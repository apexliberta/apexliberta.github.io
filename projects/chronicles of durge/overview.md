# Overview

## Project Metadata

**Author:** Liberta
**Genres:** Dark Fantasy, Psychological Horror, Epic Fantasy, RPG-Inspired
**Themes:** Fate vs. Free Will, Bloodlust, Identity, Corruption, Memory, Redemption

**Description:**
A narrative centered on the Dark Urge, an individual struggling to maintain their humanity while battling a primal, violent instinct buried deep within their soul. As they navigate the perils of Faerûn, the story explores the tension between a preordained, bloody destiny and the desperate desire to forge a unique identity.
