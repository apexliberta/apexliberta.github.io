# Character Profile: The Protagonist (The Remade Dark Urge)

**Role:** Protagonist
**True Identity:** The Dark Urge (Original Persona) / Human Teenage Boy (Constructed Persona)
**Current Form:** Human Boy (Age 16–18), reshaped by Orin from a Dragonborn body to mask his divine presence.
**Physical Condition:** "Weakened State" (50% of a normal human's strength). Lacks heavy lifting capability and traditional combat talent.

## Core Identity & Transformation
* **Original Essence:** The Dark Urge (a primordial, violent force).
* **Current Vessel:** A human teenage boy (approx. 15–17 years old).
* **The Transformation:** Orin destroyed his original form and reshaped him into this human shape—an act of both destruction and mockery. This transformation was intended to mask his divine presence.

## Physical Appearance & The "Vessel"
* **Face & Features:** Possesses a refined, almost aristocratic elegance in his facial structure, though it is often masked by an expression of weariness or haunted uncertainty. His eyes are particularly expressive; they appear soft and empathetic when he is in control, but can take on a cold, predatory intensity during moments of extreme stress or "blackouts."
* **Hair:** Thick, dark hair (deep raven-black), often slightly disheveled from travel and the rigors of combat.
* **Build:** Lean and lithe; while currently lacking significant physical strength due to his weakened state, there is an underlying athletic grace that hints at the warrior he is training to become.
* **General Vibe:** A striking contrast between a gentle, almost fragile youth and the latent, terrifying power of a divine executioner waiting to be unleashed.
* **Internal Conflict & Body Horror:** There is a massive disconnect between his fragile human exterior and the chaotic, violent essence within. He experiences the sensation of the old self trying to break through the new skin—a constant sense of "wrongness" in being human.

## Personality & Traits
* **The Human Persona:** Meek, gentle, and often unsure of himself. This identity wants to show mercy and live a peaceful life.
* **The Dark Urge:** The original, bloodthirsty persona and divine programming of Bhaal's chosen executioner.
* **Motivation:** Initially, to find out who he is; later, to become strong enough to protect Shadowheart while managing his dual nature.

## Psychological Profile
* **Memory Status:** Damaged/Lost. He is a blank slate or has fragmented memories of his former power.
* **The Dual Consciousness:** Following the Fay Enchantress's intervention, his mind is split into two distinct, warring consciousnesses within a single vessel.
* **The Nightmare Cycle & Whispers:** Suffers from gruesome nightmares and "whispers" that masquerade as his own thoughts. 
    * **Mechanism:** An exponential increase in auditory hallucinations/commands triggered by every kill ($2^{n-1}$ whispers).
    * **Progression:** Transitions from subtle murmurs to commanding, visceral urges for cruelty and depravity as the kill count rises.
* **Hallucinated Metamorphosis:** Under stress, he vividly hallucinates thick crimson scales and claws, believing he is possessed by a monster.
* **The Struggle:** A constant battle between his human identity and the overwhelming urge to commit atrocities (e.g., defiling corpses, gore, ravaging).

## Combat & Growth
* **Initial State:** Talentless in magic and physical strength.
* **Growth Path:** Moving from a "liability" to a "protector." He may eventually use the "Dark Urge" or the "Tadpole" as a specialized, non-traditional way to fight.

## Key Themes
* **Nature vs. Nurture:** The battle between innate darkness and the new human experience.
* **Identity & Transformation:** What defines a person—their soul/essence or their physical form?
* **Darkness & Taboo:** Exploring violent, visceral, and morally complex themes through a lens of vulnerability.

## Narrative Questions to Explore
1. Why did Orin choose this specific form for him?
2. How does the world react to him (seeing a boy vs. sensing a monster)?
3. What is the ultimate goal: Reclaiming power or finding peace?
