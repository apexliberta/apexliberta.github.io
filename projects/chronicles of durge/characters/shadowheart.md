# Character Profile: Shadowheart

**Role:** Deuteragonist / Love Interest
**Age:** 20–21
**Class:** Cleric (Worshipper of Shar)
**Current Appearance:** Plain, non-descript clothing (hiding her Shar armor); recent injuries being healed.

## Personality & Traits
* **Public Persona:** Abrasive, rude, arrogant, and somewhat condescending. This stems from her upbringing in the isolation of the Temple of Shar.
* **Private Persona:** Deeply empathetic and nurturing. She uses "nurturing" as a way to process her own past trauma.
* **Combat Style:** Experienced and combat-oriented.

## Psychological Profile
* **Core Trauma:** The "Wolf Attack" memory. The terror of being a helpless child in the dark, which has left her with a deep-seated fear of wolves.
* **The "Wounded Healer":** She provides the comfort to the Protagonist that she never received herself. She is "healing" her own childhood through him.
* **The Secret:** She is hiding her true identity as a Sharan cleric and the existence of the tadpole.

## Relationships
* **The Protagonist:** A deep, slow-burn connection. She sees a kindred spirit in his darkness and finds herself becoming his "anchor."
* **The Old Couple:** She treats them with a "fake" respect, but secretly finds comfort in their domesticity.
