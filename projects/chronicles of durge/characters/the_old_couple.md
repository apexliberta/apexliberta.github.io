# Character Profile: The Old Couple (Guardians of the Dark Crypt)

**Role:** Mentors / Found Family / Quest Givers
**Location:** The Farmhouse near the Dark Crypt

## The Old Man
* **Background:** A retired Black Paladin.
* **Personality:** Muscular, wary, and skeptical of strangers. He is a man of discipline and strength.
* **Role in Story:** Acts as a combat mentor to the Protagonist. He is the one who realizes the Protagonist's "talentlessness" but still provides the tools (sword/dagger) for him to protect Shadowheart.
* **Secret:** He is a sworn guardian of the Dark Crypt, tasked with watching over it in his retirement.

## The Old Woman
* **Personality:** Kind, strong-willed, and maternal. 
* **Connection to Protagonist:** She sees her deceased son in him, creating a deep, emotional maternal bond.
* **Connection to Shadowheart:** She sees her own younger, lost self in Shadowheart. She is the first to "see through" Shadowheart's attitude to the girl underneath.
* **Role in Story:** Provides the "domestic sanctuary" and the emotional heart of the first arc. She is the one who encourages the "elopement" lie.
